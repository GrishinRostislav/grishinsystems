I often see technicians working on patch panels, labeling wall jacks, and testing cables before connecting active equipment.

But surprisingly, many of them still do not use cable testers with multiple locator remotes.

Even though this can speed up cable testing and labeling several times over.

![Network Tester Locator Remotes](/images/NetworkTesterRemote.png)

### 📊 Simple Example from a Recent Project

Consider a **6-floor building** where each floor had:
* 4 cameras
* 2 access points

Using the standard testing method, a technician would need to go up to each floor at least 6 times.

In total, that is around **36 trips** through the building just for cable testing and labeling.

At that point, the technician is either running around like crazy because the project needs to be finished urgently, or slowly crawling those same 36 trips because there is simply no energy left after constantly going up and down the building.

### 💡 The Solution: Multiple Locator Remotes

Using a tester with multiple locator remotes can reduce the number of trips by **5–8 times**.

This means:
* Less wasted time
* Less physical exhaustion
* Faster project completion
* Lower labor costs for the company

This becomes even more noticeable on large projects.

For example, in a **30-floor hotel**, such a simple tool could easily save a couple of working days.
