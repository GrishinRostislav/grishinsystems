Today I noticed how much time technicians lose during CCTV installs just going up and down ladders.

Not because of configuration.

Just because tools, bits, connectors or hardware are missing at the top.

![Technician Tool Belt](/images/belt.jpg)

I almost never see low voltage or network techs using properly organized tool belts.

But honestly, having everything on you:

* drill,
* bits,
* RJ45 tools,
* anchors,
* labels,
* screws,
* even the camera itself,

can massively speed up the workflow on larger projects.

Small operational improvements save a crazy amount of labor hours over time.
